Method

These labels are deterministic wording rules, not hidden-intent detection.

What is counted: explicit language such as “what if,” “compare,” “under this constraint,” or “as a result.” A single turn can hold several labels.

What is not claimed: diagnosis, IQ, ability, personality, employability, medical status, or a causal explanation for a pattern.
